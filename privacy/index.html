1. Who we are

Mission U EMS is an EMS training studio at 1523 N La Brea Ave, Suite 202, 2nd Floor, Los Angeles, CA. The studio is run by MISSION U LLC ("Mission U", "we", "us"). This policy covers this website, the online EMS waiver and the client Nutrition Review questionnaire.

2. What we collect

When you book or message us. The "Book online", "Text us" and "WhatsApp" buttons open Calendly, your messaging app or WhatsApp. This website does not collect anything when you press them. What you then send us (for example your name, phone number, email, the time you choose and your messages) reaches us through those services, which handle it under their own privacy policies.

The Nutrition Review questionnaire. This form is only for Mission U clients with a personal access code. If you fill it in, we collect:

  • contact details: name, email, phone (optional), how you prefer to be contacted;
  • date of birth and sex;
  • your goals and what might get in the way;
  • body measurements: height, weight, waist and, if you know them, body fat and muscle mass, and recent weight changes;
  • health information: medical conditions, medications and supplements, allergies, pregnancy or breastfeeding, diets prescribed by a doctor, digestion, sleep, stress, energy and menstrual cycle;
  • activity: training, steps, type of work and schedule;
  • eating habits, food preferences, cooking and budget, and a few questions about your relationship with food;
  • what you ate on one typical day;
  • your consents, the name you type as a signature, the date and time you submitted, and the version of the form;
  • your access code and a random device number (see "On your device" below).

If you choose to reply to our confirmation email with meal photos or a body-composition scan, we receive those too.

The EMS waiver. Before your first EMS session you sign our EMS waiver online and answer health screening questions. Section 8 explains what we collect there.

On your device. While you fill in the questionnaire, your answers are saved in your own browser (local storage) so you don't lose your progress. They stay on your device until you submit the form or press "Clear saved answers". The form also keeps a random device number in your browser, which we use only to limit wrong access-code attempts. This website does not use cookies, ads, analytics or tracking pixels.

Technical logs. The company that hosts this website may keep short technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of visit) to keep the site working and secure. We do not use them to identify or profile visitors.

3. How we use it

  • to book and run your sessions and answer your messages;
  • to decide whether EMS training is right for you and keep a record of your signed waiver;
  • to prepare your Nutrition Review (your direction, calorie and macro targets and your PDF guide) and talk it through with you;
  • to send you a confirmation that your form arrived, and your review;
  • to email you tips and updates, only if you ticked that box (reply "unsubscribe" at any time and we will stop);
  • to keep our forms secure, to meet legal obligations and to protect our legal rights.

We do not send marketing text messages. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising (ads based on your activity on other websites). We do not use your information to make automated decisions about you.

4. Who can see it

  • Your health answers (questionnaire and waiver screening): only the Mission U staff who prepare your review or check your waiver.
  • Service providers. We use Google services (Gmail, Google Sheets and Google Apps Script) in Mission U's Google account to receive, store and email your forms. Google handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • The company that hosts this website, to deliver the pages to you.
  • Services you choose to use: Calendly, WhatsApp, your phone carrier and Instagram, under their own privacy policies.
  • When the law requires it, for example a valid court order, or when needed to protect someone's safety or to defend a legal claim.
  • If the studio is sold or reorganized, the new owner may receive client records, and this policy will continue to apply to them.

5. Where it is stored and how we protect it

Your answers are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and saved in a Google Sheets spreadsheet in Mission U's Google account, which only authorized Mission U staff can open. We receive a short email notice at info@mission-u-ems.com with your name, your email and a link to your answers. Your health answers are not included in that notice.

We protect the account with a strong password and two-step verification, and we limit who can open your information. No system is perfectly secure. If a security breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as California law requires.

Mission U is a fitness studio, not a healthcare provider or health plan, so the federal HIPAA rules do not apply to us. We still treat your health information as sensitive, as this policy describes.

6. How long we keep it

  • Nutrition questionnaire, your review and your PDF guide: up to 24 months after your last review, then deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Signed EMS waiver and screening answers: 4 years after your last visit (see section 8).
  • Saved answers on your device: until you submit the form or press "Clear saved answers".
  • Bookings and messages: as long as you are a client and we need them to serve you, then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

7. Health information

The questionnaire asks about your health because a useful nutrition review depends on it. We treat this as sensitive personal information. We collect it only with your separate consent (a box that is not ticked in advance) on the last step of the form, and we use it only to prepare and discuss your Nutrition Review.

  • We do not sell it, share it for advertising or use it to show you ads.
  • Only the Mission U staff who prepare your review can see it.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing info@mission-u-ems.com. We will then stop using your health information and delete it. Withdrawing does not affect reviews already completed.
  • The Nutrition Review is general nutrition guidance, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

8. EMS waiver and health screening

Before your first EMS session, we ask you to sign our EMS waiver online and answer health screening questions. We collect your name, email, phone number (optional), your answers to the screening questions, whether you agreed to photos and video, the date and time you signed, the version of the waiver, and technical details of your browser (such as browser type and time zone) so that we can confirm the signature is yours. We use this information only to decide whether EMS training is safe for you, to keep a record of your signed waiver, and to defend legal claims. Your answers are saved in Mission U's Google Sheets spreadsheet, which only authorized Mission U staff can open. Your confirmation email includes a copy of the waiver and your answers; the notice we receive does not include your health answers. We keep signed waivers for 4 years after your last visit, because we may need them if a legal claim arises, and then delete them. You can ask for a copy at any time at info@mission-u-ems.com. If you ask us to delete your waiver while you still train with us, we will not be able to continue training you.

9. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we have about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information (except a signed waiver we must keep, as section 8 explains);
  • stop sending you tips and updates.

Email info@mission-u-ems.com from the address you used in the form, or tell us at the studio. To protect you, we may ask you to confirm a few details before we share or delete anything. We answer within 45 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. You can also ask someone you authorize to make a request for you; we may ask for proof that you gave them permission.

10. California residents

Some California privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), apply only to larger businesses. Whether or not a particular law applies to us, we give every client the rights in section 9, we do not sell or share personal information, and we use sensitive information only to provide the service you asked for.

Under California's "Shine the Light" law (Civil Code § 1798.83), you can ask whether we share personal information with other companies for their direct marketing. We do not.

11. Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control and other websites

This website does not track visitors over time or across other websites, and it does not let other companies do so through our pages. It works the same way whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale and sharing of your information; since we do not sell or share it, nothing on the site changes.

Links on this site to Calendly, WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Maps and news articles take you to other websites. Their own privacy policies apply there.

12. Children

This website, the EMS waiver and the Nutrition Review are for adults. EMS training at Mission U is for clients 21 and older, and the questionnaire cannot be submitted by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use health information you have already given us, we will ask for your consent again before using it in the new way.

14. Contact us

Questions or requests about your information: info@mission-u-ems.com.
