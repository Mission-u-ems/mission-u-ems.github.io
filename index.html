Contraindications

Before you start

For adults 21 and older.

EMS is not for everyone. Please read this before your first session.

Absolute

EMS training is not permitted

  • Pacemaker or other electronic implants
  • Metal implants in the stimulation area
  • Epilepsy
  • Pregnancy
  • Recent surgery (less than 3 months ago)

Relative

Doctor’s approval required

  • Cardiovascular diseases, arrhythmia, hypertension
  • Asthma
  • Cancer (current or past)
  • Inguinal hernias
  • Varicose veins, thrombosis
  • Diabetes
  • Neurological or autoimmune disorders
  • Recent injuries or surgeries
  • Open wounds, inflammation, burns, or skin conditions in the stimulation area

FAQ

Good questions

Is EMS safe?

For healthy adults, EMS is generally well tolerated when done with a trained coach and the right intensity. It is not for everyone, and like any workout it carries some risk. Every client fills in a health screening before the first session, and we start gently. Please check the contraindications first.

How often should I train?

1–2 sessions per week, with at least 48 hours between sessions.

Does it hurt?

It should never feel painful. Your trainer adjusts the intensity to your comfort.

Is it good for beginners?

Yes. Every session is customized to your fitness level.

What should I wear?

We provide the EMS suit. Athletic shoes are recommended, barefoot is allowed.

When will I see results?

Many people feel their muscles working from the first session. Visible changes usually take weeks of regular training together with good nutrition, and they differ from person to person.
