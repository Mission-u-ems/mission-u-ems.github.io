Mission U at Strength in the City LA

On October 17, 2026, we packed our EMS devices, a suit and a table sign that said “Test your EMS level” and set up a booth at the Strength in the City wellness festival in Hollywood. Here is how the day went.

Most people have heard of EMS. Far fewer have felt it. A festival is a good place to fix that: people come to move, try new things and ask questions. So when Strength in the City came to Los Angeles, we brought the studio to them.

The festival

Strength in the City started in Chicago and now runs in cities across the U.S. Its founder, Liezyl Ribeiro, told Athletech News she wants it to be “the South by Southwest (SXSW) for wellness.”3

The LA edition took place on Saturday, October 17, 2026, at Academy LA on Hollywood Boulevard, from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. The organizers describe it as a community-driven health and wellness festival with more than 20 fitness and wellness classes, local vendors, brands and recovery services, and a free community 5K run or 2-mile walk in the morning.1,2

Our booth

We set up under a dark tent on the lawn. On the table: the Mission U cloth, a QR code to book a session and a small sign that said it plainly: Test your EMS level. Next to it stood a mannequin wearing a full EMS suit, so people could see what a real session looks like. The EMS devices, built into rolling cases, did the actual work. A screen behind the table played our studio videos.

How a try-on worked

Nobody had to change clothes. Guests put on EMS cuffs over the arms, Ella checked the fit and set the intensity on the device. Then came simple moves: biceps curls with light dumbbells, a squat, a bent-over row. One guest tried pads on her back, another did squats side by side with Ella.

The idea is the same as in the studio, only shorter. The impulse makes the muscle contract, you add a movement on top, and the trainer stays next to you the whole time, adjusting the level to how it feels. Judging by the faces in our photos, the first impulse often ended in a laugh.

The questions that follow

After a first try, the usual questions come up. Does it hurt? Is it safe? How often should I train? What do I wear? A booth is a good place to start that conversation, but the honest answers depend on the person. That is why every first session at Mission U begins with a short check of your health and goals, and the intensity is set to your comfort. Our FAQ covers the basics.

See you at the next one

Thank you to the Strength in the City team and to everyone who stopped by, put on a cuff and gave it a try. If you felt your first impulse at our booth and want the full 20-minute session in a suit, the studio is in Hollywood, on La Brea. Your first visit is $60.
